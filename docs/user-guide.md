# Job Hunt Hub: user guide

## Setup wizard (first run, about 3 minutes)

1. **Welcome:** what the app does, and what it never does.
2. **Your resume:** paste your resume text (or a .txt or .md file). Claude copies your facts into fields and flags any number it couldn't find in what you pasted. This becomes your master profile.
3. **Your targets:** titles, locations, work mode, salary floor, notice period and resume page size (A4 or Letter).
4. **Inbox and calendar:** optionally turn on Gmail and Google Calendar (read-only), then press **Test connections**. Claude asks you to allow each connector once.
5. **Follow-ups:** how many days after applying, and after an interview, before a nudge is due.
6. **First role:** paste a job post. Claude fills the card and scores the match.

A short tour follows. Run setup or the tour again from **Settings**.

## Board

- One column per stage: Saved, Applied, Screening, Interview, Offer. Rejected and withdrawn roles fold into **Closed**.
- Drag a card to move it, or open it and pick a stage. On a phone, tap a stage chip to see its list.
- Cards with a follow-up due have a blue edge and appear in the strip at the top.

## Inside a role

| Tab        | What you get                                                                                                                     |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Overview   | All fields, notes, the post text, the stage timeline, **I heard back today**                                                     |
| Analysis   | Must-haves, nice-to-haves, red flags, talking points, and a match score backed by quotes from the post                           |
| Resume     | **Tailor my resume**, a diff against your master profile, every refused change, a one-page preview, and PDF, DOCX or text export |
| Cover note | A 120 to 180 word note from the same facts, with warnings if it drifts                                                           |
| Follow-up  | A drafted email to copy and send yourself, then **I sent it**                                                                    |

## Inbox

**Sync Gmail** searches the last 45 days for confirmations, assessments, interview invites, offers and rejections. Each match becomes a suggestion ("Move Fabrikam to Interview?"). Nothing changes until you press **Accept**. Emails with no matching card can become a new card.

Upcoming interviews come from calendar events containing "interview" in the next 14 days.

## How the resume stays honest

- Claude may choose, order and lightly rephrase your own bullets. That's all.
- Every rewrite is checked: a number not in the original bullet, a skill not in your profile, or a bullet id that doesn't exist is refused. Your original line is kept and the refusal is listed.
- One column, standard headings, real text, no tables or images. The PDF is verified to be exactly one page. If content runs long, the font steps down, then the oldest bullets drop.

## Desktop extras

In the installed app (**Settings, Desktop**):

- Notifications for due follow-ups and interviews in the next 36 hours.
- A menu bar count of follow-ups due, with one-click links into each role.
- **Import from a tracker file:** reads a markdown table (Company, Role, Stage, Link columns) from a file on your computer. This needs full computer access, which the app asks for once.

## Job sites

The app can't open web pages from inside Claude, so paste the post text when you add a role.
